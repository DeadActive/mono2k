MONO2K Firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1 V0.1 — Browser emulator

Six mono machines

A Monomachine-style synthesizer for a palm-sized FM synth. Six tracks, each one machine: sine, noise, chip, two kinds of FM, supersaw, chord, digital waves, a voice. A 64-step sequencer with parameter locks, trig conditions and ratchets. A bus that breaks the sound beautifully.

    02

    Machines

    Nine machines in six families, after what is published about the Monomachine's: written from scratch for MONO2K, integer only, to be tuned against the original by ear and by measurement. Point at a card: its mark turns, as on the screen.

    03

    Sequencer

    Four projects of four banks (A–D) of sixteen patterns, each pattern a whole song part with its own six machines, sounds and bus, as on the modern Elektron boxes. SAVE keeps a pattern, GLO + SAVE reloads it, SAVE held keeps the project; GLO + REC, PLAY and SEQ copy, paste and clear (a pattern on HOME, a track's sound on its pages). Six tracks of up to 64 steps, on four pages of sixteen. Every step is a trig with its note, accent and slide. Hold a step and turn any knob: that value is locked to the step and comes back to the kit on the next trig. In the lanes, a locked step is checked.

    Modern trig conditions: a chance (75, 50, 33, 25, 12%), every A of B loops (1:2 … 4:4), the first loop only, after the previous condition (PRE), or only when you hold FILL. A chance is drawn as its own density of dither. Ratchets repeat a step two to four times.

    04

    Destroy

    Every track: the Monomachine's chain, with HP and LP filters as a base and a width, distortion, a parametric EQ band, sample rate reduction and a delay of its own in 8-bit mu-law at half rate. Then bits, a wavefold and an XOR that mangles the samples.

    The bus: an SPU-style reverb at 22 kHz, a beat repeat that slices the last sixteenth (reversed, halving, slowing to a tape stop), tape wow and flutter, a 4-bit ADPCM crunch, hiss and dust, and a drive into the soft clip.

    05

    Status

    A prototype. This page runs the firmware's C core as it is; the FM-1 build adds Felucca's platform (the hardware layer, USB, storage, the update loader and installer) and has not been flashed yet. Saves may change.

    Built on the open FM-1 work: Felucca by Leo Kuroshita (Hügelton Instruments), CS-1, X0X, FoMni and the others. Inter Tight under the SIL OFL. MONO2K is not affiliated with Elektron or M-VAVE; Monomachine is Elektron's.
