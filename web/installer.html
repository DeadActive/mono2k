How it will work

MONO2K will install as Felucca does, the open FM-1 firmware it is built on, with an installer adapted from Felucca's.

  1. Connect the FM-1 to a computer with a USB cable that carries data, not one for charging only.
  2. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on the computer, and allow MIDI when the browser asks: the installer talks to the FM-1 over USB-MIDI, with SysEx.
  3. The installer backs up first: the FM-1's own songs and settings, saved as a file on the computer.
  4. Press Install. The running firmware stages an update loader, and the loader writes MONO2K. Keep the cable in and the page open until it says it is done.
  5. The FM-1 starts again, into MONO2K.

Back to the official firmware

M-VAVE's own updater takes the FM-1 back to its official firmware at any time. The installer will also offer to: with M-VAVE's unmodified firmware file, checked in your browser and sent nowhere, after a backup.
